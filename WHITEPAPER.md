## 5. rutaX — Proof of Activity

> *Move. Earn. Evolve.*

rutaX is the central mobile application of the DACnetwork ecosystem and the foundation of the Proof-of-Activity (PoA) mechanism. It represents the primary onboarding layer for non-technical users and the main source of $DAC token generation.

rutaX is built on the premise that real human activity has measurable economic value and should be transparently recognized in the digital economy.

**Generation Mechanism**

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Base formula | 1 $DAC per 10 km walked (GPS verified) |
| Daily limit | Maximum 10 $DAC / user / day |
| Blockchain | Solana (Token 2022 Standard) |
| Validation | GPS tracking + anti-fraud algorithms |
| Availability | Mobile app iOS & Android |

**Anti-Fraud System**

Integrity of the PoA mechanism is ensured through multiple validation layers:
- **Speed verification** — detects transport movement (speed > 20 km/h invalidated)
- **Altitude validation** — detects trajectory anomalies
- **Route analysis** — identifies suspicious patterns or repetitive routes without variation
- **GPS anomaly detection** — identifies GPS spoofing and simulations
- **Machine learning anti-pattern** — behavioral detection model in continuous evolution

**Democratizing Value Generation**

Traditional mining requires: high-performance hardware, initial capital, and energy resources.

rutaX replaces these with: universal access to movement, technological validation, and individual participation — no intermediaries, no financial barriers.

> ***DACnetwork is not a speculative crypto project. It is an ecosystem focused on measurable value and universal access.***

## 10. DePIN Integration

DACnetwork integrates a DePIN (Decentralized Physical Infrastructure Network) model through DAC Smart Systems. DePIN represents a new paradigm where distributed physical infrastructure is coordinated and rewarded through blockchain mechanisms.

> ***DePIN connects the physical world with the digital economy through transparent blockchain validation.***

**How DePIN works in DACnetwork:**
- Physical DAC Smart Systems devices act as network nodes
- Infrastructure contributions (uptime, validated events) become measurable on-chain
- Decentralization extends beyond software systems into the physical world
- Physical nodes contribute to contextual validation of ecosystem activity
- $DAC rewards for uptime and verified infrastructure contributions

**Proof of Infrastructure**

The third contribution model in DACnetwork, alongside Proof of Activity and Proof of Creation. Proof of Infrastructure rewards participants who provide and maintain the physical infrastructure of the ecosystem — devices that operate locally without mandatory cloud dependency, contributing uptime and validated events while preserving user data sovereignty.
