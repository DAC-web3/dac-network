# Contributing to DACnetwork

Thank you for your interest in contributing to DACnetwork!

We are building an open decentralized civilization — and every contribution matters.

---

## How to Contribute

### Option 1 — Fill in our Collaboration Form
The easiest way to get started. Tell us who you are and how you want to help.

👉 [DACnetwork Collaboration Form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdBRhcaV6d-CBqXNo0QDP4H6J55wKb8RLkSJJxTlXR7hkDqDg/viewform)

### Option 2 — Open a GitHub Issue
Already on GitHub? Open a Collaboration Request directly:

👉 [Open a Collaboration Request](https://github.com/DAC-web3/dac-network/issues/new/choose)

- Genuine interest in decentralized technology
- Willingness to build in public
- Contribution before accumulation
- Long-term thinking over short-term gain
